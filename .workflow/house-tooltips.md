# House-styled tooltips (global title delegate)

Context: no work item (direct session, director screenshot: the native gray title box clashed with the dark aesthetic). Branch: main. Status: current. Repo: agentic-workflow-designer.

## Current behavior

- ONE document-level delegate restyles every title-attribute tooltip in the app: on hover it moves the element's `title` into `data-tip` (suppressing the native box before its delay can fire; DOM rebuilt by renders gets a fresh title and the same treatment), and after 350ms shows `#appTip` - a house-styled box (surface background, border, radius, shadow, 340px max width, pre-line wrapping) positioned under the element, flipped above and viewport-clamped when needed. Mouseout before the delay cancels; any mousedown or scroll hides instantly. When the title move would strip an icon-only element's accessible name, the text is preserved as `aria-label`.
- Zero per-element changes: hundreds of existing `title=` hints (buttons, toggles, monitor rows, preset items, chips) inherit the styling automatically, and future `title=` markup keeps working unchanged. SVG `<title>` CHILD elements (the canvas node dots) are a different mechanism and stay native - acceptable, noted here.

## Why and scope

Tooltips carry a large share of the product's guidance (the density doctrine deliberately moved depth into them), so the native OS box was the most-seen off-aesthetic surface in the app. A delegate matches the single-file philosophy: one block, removable, no markup churn. Scope: one CSS rule, one JS block, two pins. Non-goals: no per-element migration, no SVG-title replacement, no touch-device behavior (native titles never showed there anyway).
