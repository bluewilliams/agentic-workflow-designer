# Epic-run feedback: nine findings from a real two-repo run, integrated

Context: no work item (a designer-generated workflow ran a real two-repo epic end to end; the director asked the run's agent for improvement feedback and approved integration of the safe items). Branch: main. Status: current. Repo: agentic-workflow-designer.

## Current behavior

- **Execution model is capability-conditional.** One-shot stays the BASELINE contract in the Sub-Agents beats. A harness that can message a completed agent is told to CONTINUE the existing agent for revise cycles and delta re-reviews with a surgical brief. Re-spawn (pointed at the memory file) is the stated fallback, never the preferred path where continuation exists. The revise tooltip matches. Evidence: the run's delta re-review on a continued agent cost a fraction of a fresh full pass.
- **The memory file is the authoritative report channel.** The orchestrator beats (Sub-Agents block and the shared protocol) instruct: on step completion, read the step's `@slug.md` complete handoff FIRST. The inline result may be a digest, truncated, or lost; the file never is. Do not re-spawn a step whose file shows completed work. Evidence: five of five steps delivered by disk; zero delivered inline.
- **Revise routing targets only the faulted agents.** The drawn revise targets remain the canvas truth. The orchestrator routes feedback ONLY to the target(s) the verdict's findings name, and the revise brief must name approved parallel branches and forbid touching their work. Evidence: the gate faulted one of two parallel branches; blanket re-spawn would have invited damage to approved work.
- **The orchestrator logs its own decisions to `shared.md`** (`## @orchestrator | ts | decision` at every gate verdict, clarify answer, and revise dispatch). Evidence: agent state recovered perfectly after the run; orchestrator decision history existed nowhere in memory.
- **Testers prove discrimination.** The tester template requires: break the guarded thing, confirm the test FAILS, revert, re-verify, report the failure output. The reviewer's tests dimension asks the same question and corroborates at least one claim. Evidence: the run's tester did this spontaneously and it was the run's single most valuable behavior.
- **Do-not-fake and machine-contention beats.** Tester and validator: an impossible check is never simulated or reported passing - write the manual procedure and say it did not run. Before full suite runs, note machine load; re-run timing-sensitive failures in isolation. Evidence: three checks were honestly unrun; three timing flakes appeared under exhausted swap and passed in isolation.
- **The record spec routes durable content out of scaffolding.** Resume/handoff says what belongs elsewhere (unrun-check procedures go to Verify). Finalize sweeps stripped sections for stranded durable content BEFORE stripping, and the completion gate checks the sweep. Evidence: five manual verification procedures were nearly deleted by finalize.
- **Environment capability survey, once.** The first step to touch the record writes what the environment can and cannot verify into Verify; planner and architect templates carry the survey-once beat; downstream steps inherit and may not silently skip a check by assuming impossibility. Evidence: three agents rediscovered the same limits serially.

## Why and scope

Every change is run-evidenced - the integration gate the parked checkpoint change lacked. Not integrated: nothing; all nine findings landed (finding 9 lives in the designer's emitted protocol, its correct home for generated workflows). The run's three protected conventions (DONE/STATUS transcription, fan-out write rule, clarify gate) were left untouched by design.
